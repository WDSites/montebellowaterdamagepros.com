Crawl space safety before anyone goes under the house

The space is tight, dark and often wet, so the safest first step is to look from the access door without climbing in. Shine a flashlight along the floor. If you see standing water, wiring close to it or a sagging floor above, stay out.

Do not enter a wet crawl space to reach anything, and shut off the crawl space circuit only from a dry panel. Call the utility or 911 for any electrical or gas hazard, and leave the house if you smell gas. Never enter a wet crawl space alone, because low clearance, sharp debris and pests add risk. Close the main water valve if a pipe is running.

How water ends up beneath a raised-floor house

A raised-floor house sits on a perimeter foundation with a gap below the wood floor framing. Water arrives by one of four routes: a leaking supply line, a cracked or loose drain, rain that runs toward the foundation or an open vent or damaged access door. Each route leaves a different pattern.

The 2023 American Community Survey puts the median year Montebello homes were built at 1963. The Earthquake Brace + Bolt program from the California Residential Mitigation Program targets wood-framed houses built before 1980 on a raised foundation with a crawl space or short cripple walls.

Together those facts say raised-floor homes are part of the local mix. We cannot give a count of Montebello crawl space homes. If you are unsure about yours, find the access door and the foundation vents now, before you need them. A house on a slab belongs with the slab leak water damage page.

What the floor and the air above are telling you

Water under the house often shows itself upstairs before anyone looks below. A spongy floor, boards cupping at the edges or doors that start sticking can point to damp framing.

Smell is the other tell. A musty odor that is stronger near floor registers suggests moisture below. Mold needs moisture, so a lasting odor is a reason to get a reading rather than spray something. If growth is visible, mold remediation follows once the space is dry.

  • Floors that feel soft, bouncy or cupped
  • Insulation sagging or hanging from the subfloor
  • Musty smell near floor registers
  • Dark or damp wood framing
  • Water pooled on the plastic vapor barrier

Find the source: plumber, drainage or vent

Our crews do not repair plumbing, so a leaking supply or drain line goes to a plumber first. Rain intrusion has different fixes. A contractor can redirect yard runoff, and a carpenter can repair a damaged vent or access door.

A Safe Clean Water Los Angeles planning document for a City of Montebello stormwater project names known flooding on Lincoln Avenue between Avenida De La Merced and San Gabriel Boulevard. It also lists candidate sites near Rio Hondo, Grant Rea Park and Olympic Boulevard. Street flooding there is a stormwater issue, but water that enters a building is a damage issue, so photograph it and call.

Weather service totals for downtown Los Angeles show the storm of February 4 to 6, 2024 dropped about 8.51 inches, the second-wettest three-day span on record there. The long-term annual normal is about 14.25 inches. After a storm like that, check the crawl space within a day or two, because water that finds its way in does not drain out on its own.

Pumping, drying and sealing off the space

Crews start with moisture meters on the floor framing and a thermal imaging camera from the access opening to see which joists and subfloor sections are wet. Extraction units remove standing water, and weighted extractors work low spots and soaked insulation.

Sagging batt insulation comes out because it holds water against the subfloor. Plastic sheeting and containment then separate the crawl space from the living area so moisture and dust stay in the work zone. Air movers with ducting push air across the joists while LGR or desiccant dehumidifiers pull moisture from the air.

We work to ANSI/IICRC S500 practice. Water from a leaking drain is category 2 or 3, so removal and cleaning steps change. Readings are taken at each visit, and equipment stays until they reach a dry reference.

What stretches a crawl space job

Access comes first. A tight opening slows every task and every piece of equipment that has to go through it. After that, the depth and area of the water, the amount of insulation and vapor barrier involved and the water category all change the scope.

The number of drying visits depends on readings, and wood framing dries slower than most people expect. A pipe that leaked for weeks costs more time than one that failed yesterday.

Insurance: a leak below the floor versus water from outside

Sudden and accidental pipe failure is often covered, while a gradual leak or neglect often is not. Rain that runs in from the yard can be treated differently from a burst line, and flood damage usually needs separate flood coverage.

Check your policy and ask your insurer before work starts. Photos taken from the access door and our moisture readings help show what happened and when.

Mistakes people make with a wet crawl space

Do not install new insulation over damp framing, because it traps moisture against the wood. Do not spray an odor product and call the job done either. The smell returns when the source is still wet.

A vapor barrier that looks dry does not prove the wood above it is dry. Do not run extension cords through wet areas, and do not go under the house alone to check.

Crawl space water in Montebello: what to know before it rains

Which utility serves your address is printed on your water bill, so check that before calling about the main. Look at your water bill. A leak before the meter is generally the utility's concern and one after it is the owner's, so the bill tells you who to call.

In the 2020 Census, 55.6 percent of Montebello's 20,308 housing units were renter-occupied. If you rent and the floor feels damp, write to your landlord the same day and keep a copy. Tenants usually cannot repair the space under the building, but they can document it.

Questions

Wet crawl space questions answered

Is emergency crawl space water damage cleanup available in Montebello?

Yes. Dispatch answers every night and weekend, and holidays are covered the same way. If a crew is available it heads out the same day, and no exact arrival time is promised. A plumber or contractor fixes the source.

Should I go into the crawl space to look?

No, not into standing water or near wiring. Look from the access door with a flashlight and photograph what you see. Our crew has the gear and the training for the confined space.

Will you need to cut into my floor?

Not usually. Work starts from the access opening. If a floor above has cupped or soaked, the crew may dry it from above too, and the readings decide whether anything must be removed.

How long will the space stay wet?

It depends on readings. Joists and subfloor dry slowly in a closed space, so the crew sets the drying targets by readings, not by a calendar. Wood framing can need longer.

Is rain under the house covered by insurance?

It depends on how the water got in. Sudden pipe failure is often covered, gradual leaks often are not, and flood damage usually needs separate coverage. Your policy wording decides, so ask the insurer before you assume either way.

Can you tell me how many Montebello homes have crawl spaces?

No. We have no local count and will not invent one. Check your own foundation for vents and an access door. Your inspector or the original plans will also show it.
