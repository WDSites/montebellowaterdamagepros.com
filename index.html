Why Montebello calls us

Water damage restoration done in a set order

Restoration works when it follows the published industry standards, ANSI/IICRC S500 for water damage and S520 for mold. Those documents set out how water is categorized, how drying is monitored and when a material is dried in place or removed. Any company you hire should be able to tell you whether its crews work to them, and you should ask.

The same order runs on every call. First the source is stopped, then water is removed, then the structure is dried and checked with moisture meters, and only then does rebuilding start. Skipping ahead is how a repaired wall ends up hiding a wet stud.

That order also protects you in a claim. Moisture readings taken before and after drying show an insurer what was wet and what was dried, which is far stronger than a photo of a finished wall.

Your first hour

What to do while you wait for the crew

The minutes before a crew arrives matter. Water moves into drywall, insulation and flooring faster than most people expect, and wet drywall can begin to swell and soften within hours. A few safe steps slow that down.

Stop the source

Close the fixture shut-off valve or the main water valve. If you do not know where the main valve is, find it now, because a stuck or hidden valve is a common surprise during an emergency.

Think about power first

If water reaches outlets, appliances or the panel, stay out. Cut power only if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely. For any electrical or gas risk, call the utility or 911 before calling us.

Protect what you can lift

Move valuables, electronics, rugs and small furniture out of the wet area. Put foil or plastic under furniture legs that must stay.

Keep people and pets out

If the water came from a sewer backup, a toilet overflow or flooding, treat it as contaminated. Do not wade through it and do not run fans that spread it.

Local knowledge

Water damage in Montebello: what the city's layout means for you

Montebello was incorporated on October 16, 1920, and renamed that year from Newmark, the original townsite. The city covers about 8.37 square miles roughly 8 miles east of downtown Los Angeles, with the Rio Hondo along its east side. If you live or work near the river, look up your address on the FEMA Flood Map Service Center, because flood damage is usually excluded from standard policies and flood coverage is a separate purchase.

The 2020 Census counted 20,308 housing units in Montebello, and 55.6 percent were renter-occupied. That matters in a loss. A tenant often cannot choose the repair contractor, but a tenant can document damage, report it in writing and call a 24/7 line to keep water from spreading while the landlord and insurer sort out responsibility.

Water service is split too. The City of Montebello lists four providers: California Water Service, Montebello Land & Water Company, San Gabriel Valley Water Company with the City of Montebello Water Department, and South Montebello Irrigation District. Check your water bill so you know who to call if a service line or meter leaks before the water reaches your house.

Questions

Common questions

How do I know if I have water damage in Montebello or just a stain?

A dry stain stays the same size and feels hard. Water damage keeps changing. Look for a ring that grows, paint that bubbles, trim that lifts, a soft spot in drywall or a musty smell that returns. If you are not sure, call the 24/7 line and describe it. A moisture meter reading settles the question faster than guessing.

Do you handle water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Montebello?

Yes. The dispatch line never closes, so nights, weekends and holidays are covered. Same-day response is offered when a crew is available. Meanwhile stop the source if you can, shut off the main water valve, and keep people and pets away from water that might be contaminated.

Will my insurance cover the water damage?

It depends on your policy and on how the water got there. Sudden and accidental damage, such as a burst supply line, is often covered. Slow leaks and neglect often are not, and flood damage usually needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy and ask your insurer. We document the loss with photos and a written scope so you have records for your claim.

What does restoration cost, and when will I know the price?

Cost depends on the water category, the square footage affected, the materials involved, how many days of drying equipment are needed and how much rebuilding follows. The scope and price are explained before work starts, so you can decide with real numbers. For a real figure on your property, call the 24/7 line.

Should I call a restoration company or my landlord first?

If you rent, do both. More than half of Montebello homes are renter-occupied, so many losses start in a unit the occupant does not own. Tell the landlord or property manager right away and photograph everything. Calling the restoration line at the same time keeps water from spreading while the paperwork happens.
